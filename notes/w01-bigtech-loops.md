# Google Hiring Process: Application to Offer

Source: Google Careers "Our hiring process" page. Anything the page does not say is marked **not stated**.

## 1. Stages from application to offer

Typical timeline: roughly **6-8 weeks** (varies by role and team).

| # | Stage | What happens |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Resume** | Tailor a resume to each job. Show alignment with the minimum qualifications (MQs), use data, and keep it short. Cover letters are not required. See [Create a Careers profile](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/profile). |
| 2 | **Research** | Learn about Google: [about.google](https://about.google/), [team sites](https://careers.google.com/teams/), [locations](https://careers.google.com/locations/), [benefits](https://careers.google.com/benefits/). |
| 3 | **Apply online** | Limit of **3 applications every 30 days**. [Explore open roles](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs), [set up job alerts](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/alerts?q=project%20manager). |
| 4 | **Assessments** (may apply) | Google Hiring Assessment (workstyle skills) and possibly role-related assessments such as a coding exercise. |
| 5 | **Recruiter conversations** | One or two shorter phone or video calls covering key skills, team info, and your preferences (e.g., location). |
| 6 | **Project work** (role-dependent) | Case study, writing sample, or code sample before interviews. |
| 7 | **Interviews** | A panel of interviews, by video or in person, with a rotating cast of Googlers. Structured rubrics and open-ended questions. No brainteasers. |
| 8 | **Decision** | Perspectives from the application and interviews are brought together for review. |
| 9 | **Offer** | The recruiter presents the offer, compensation structure, and [benefits](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/benefits). [Global mobility support](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/benefits/mobility) is available for other locations. |
| 10 | **Onboarding** | Badging, insurance, etc., then you become a "Noogler". |


What the page does say:
- Interviews are "a panel of interviews, either over video or in person." The number of rounds is not given.
- Every candidate is assessed with the same clear rubrics for that role (structured interviewing).
- Questions are role-related and open-ended, focused on problem solving, teamwork, and strengths.
- Accommodations (sign language interpreters, specialized equipment, breaks, extended time) are available: [Accommodations at Google](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/stories/accommodations-at-google/).
- Prep: [How to prepare for interviews](https://careers.google.com/interview-tips/).




# Meta Hiring Process: Application to Offer

Source: Meta "Interviewing at Meta" page. Anything the page does not say is marked **not stated**.
Note: the text provided contains no URLs, so pages are named but not linked (careers site, culture page, FAQ, Career Profile).

## 1. Stages from application to offer

Timeline: **not stated**.

| # | Stage | What happens |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Prepare** | Update your resume and online job profiles. The careers site accepts **only one resume per applicant**, so write it to cover the positions you want. Read about Meta's culture, mission, values and principles. |
| 2 | **Apply** | Search the careers site and confirm you meet all minimum qualifications, with your resume clearly showing it. |
| 3 | **Initial call** | For most roles, a call with a recruiter covering your background, experience, long-term goals and interest. The recruiter shares details on the role and hiring team. **Select roles:** the first step may be a prescreen form via Career Profile. |
| 4 | **Interview rounds** | Multiple rounds assessing technical background and problem-solving. Initial interviews are typically by phone or video, with peers, cross-functional partners and leaders. You can send personalized thank-you messages to each interviewer through your career profile. |
| 5 | **Offer** | If selected, the recruiter sends an offer letter. Whether or not you get an offer, the recruiter tells you your application status. |
| 6 | **Onboarding** | If you accept, your up-to-date onboarding checklist appears in your career profile. |

What the page does say:
- The process "typically involves multiple rounds of interviews" with no exact number given.
- Depending on the role, tasks may include writing code, solving algorithms, or creating a case study.
- Initial interviews are by phone or video. An onsite stage is not mentioned.
- Prep tips: review the preparation information from your recruiter and in your career profile, research your interviewers, and test your computer and WiFi beforehand.

## 4. Team matching: before or after the offer

# Amazon Hiring Process: Application to Offer

Source: Amazon.jobs "How we hire" pages (online application, assessments, phone screening, interview loop). The process differs from role to role. Anything the pages do not say is marked **not stated**.

## 1. Stages from application to offer

Overall timeline: **not stated**. Response times are given for some steps (below).

| # | Stage | What happens |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Online application** | [Search jobs](https://www.amazon.jobs/search) and click "Apply Now." Create or log into a profile. Details: [Online application](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/online-application). No cover letters are accepted. A resume is recommended, but if you have none you must give a brief description of your background, experience (with dates), education and skills. Warehouse/fulfillment center roles need no resume: [fulfillment center hiring process](https://hiring.amazon.com/hiring-process). |
| 2 | **Assessments** (role-dependent) | Online, usually remote. **Work style assessments** (10-20 min) are based on the Leadership Principles. **Work sample simulations** (20 min to 1 hour) are virtual job-related tasks. Some have deadlines with no extensions. Must be completed solely by the applicant. Details: [Assessments](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/assessments). |
| 3 | **Phone screening** (certain roles) | Behavioral-based questions guided by the [Leadership Principles](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles), over Zoom or Amazon Chime. Some teams add role-specific exercises or online assessments. The recruiter tries to reply within **two business days**. Details: [Phone screening](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/phone-screening). |
| 4 | **Interview loop** | Individual meetings with current employees, remote or in person. Details: [Interview loop](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/interview-loop). |
| 5 | **After the loop** | A feedback survey arrives by email. Expect to hear back within **five business days**. Travel expense instructions follow if you traveled. |
| 6 | **Offer** | **Not stated** on the pages provided. |

Application status is checked by logging into your application profile. Amazon says it cannot contact everyone who applies.

## 2. Onsite rounds by type and count

The loop is a series of one-on-one interviews of about **45 minutes to 1 hour each**, with **two to seven interviewers**. The pages do not say how many rounds of each type there are.

| Type | Count |
|------|-------|
| Coding | **Not stated.** Technical prep applies to tech roles (e.g., software development, machine learning): [Software development topics](https://amazon.jobs/p-software-development-topics). |
| Design | **Not stated** |
| Behavioral | **Not stated** as a number. Interviews are described as focused on behavioral-based questions tied to the Leadership Principles (STAR-style stories), with no brain teasers. |
| Other | **Not stated** as a number. Some roles ask for a **writing sample** (narrative memo, 1-6 pages) or a role-specific exercise, and the candidate is told in advance. |


Interviewers take detailed notes to share with the rest of the team, but the pages do not describe how the final decision is made.


## 5. Published rules on AI tools in interviews

**Candidate use of AI tools: not stated** on the pages provided.

What is stated:
- Some Zoom-enabled interviews use **interview transcription technology**, which is activated **only if you have consented to Amazon's AI preferences**. You can [review or update your AI preferences](https://www.amazon.jobs/en-US/user/settings). See the [interview transcription FAQs](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/faq/interview-process).
- All assessments must be completed solely by the applicant.
